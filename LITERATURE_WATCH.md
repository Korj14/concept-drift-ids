# Literature Watch

## 6 October 2026 — Stage 3B milestone refresh

A fresh adversarial search was performed immediately before System A implementation.

The search continued to find 2026 work establishing that neuro-symbolic intrusion detection itself is occupied, including:

- P. K. Badhan, *Neuro-symbolic machine learning for lightweight and interpretable IoT edge intrusion detection*, Discover Sensors, 2026, DOI: 10.1007/s44397-026-00047-z.
- Yang et al., *NeSySwarm-IDS: End-to-End Differentiable Neuro-Symbolic Logic for Privacy-Preserving Intrusion Detection in UAV Swarms*, Applied Sciences, 2026, DOI: 10.3390/app16073204.
- Lynda et al., *A quantum enhanced neuro symbolic intrusion detection system for software defined networking*, Scientific Reports, 2026, DOI: 10.1038/s41598-026-59344-7.

This milestone search did not identify a new peer-reviewed flow-level NIDS paper that collapses the governing conjunction of:

1. an explicit statistical concept-drift event as the endogenous symbolic trigger;
2. a validated and versioned symbolic rule-evolution lifecycle;
3. a matched adaptive comparator isolating symbolic evolution; and
4. longitudinal detection plus explanation-quality evaluation.

This is evidence from a finite search, not proof of nonexistence. The narrowed gap therefore remains viable at this milestone, while broad “first neuro-symbolic/adaptive/rule-updating IDS” claims remain prohibited.

The literature watch must be repeated at later major milestones and before submission.
