Before any treatment outcome for a new Stage-10 scenario or dataset is accessed, its stage-specific protocol must freeze, as applicable:

- scenario/dataset identity and scientific rationale;
- source/provenance and inclusion/exclusion criteria;
- chronology or pseudo-chronology construction;
- label semantics and target mapping;
- attack mechanism or transition mechanism;
- source rows / withheld classes / introduction rules;
- boundary/reference convention and detector interpretation;
- prevalence handling and exclusions;
- train/development/adaptation/test evidence partitions;
- preprocessing and feature semantics;
- exact seeds and paired treatment structure;
- endpoints and explanation metrics;
- information timing, label availability, and leakage controls;
- neural-update identity and exact C/D matched-isolation contract;
- symbolic generation/validation separation and lifecycle semantics;
- comparator policy;
- missing/invalid-run handling;
- primary versus secondary/diagnostic status;
- permitted inferential family and multiplicity treatment.

No stage-specific result may be viewed first and protocolized afterward.

## Selection rules

Scenario and dataset selection must be based on scientific compatibility, reconstructible flow/label semantics, and the external-validity question—not on whether preliminary outcomes are favorable.

For the second dataset:

- inclusion/exclusion criteria must be frozen before C/D outcomes;
- materially different operating context is required for an external-replication claim;
- corrected/reconstructed CICIDS variants do not count as an independent second environment;
- incompatible feature or symbolic semantics must not be forced into artificial harmonization merely to claim replication;
- if the same symbolic feature semantics cannot be defended without distortion, narrow the external-validity claim rather than fabricate comparability.

## Causal unit and reporting rules

Within each scenario/dataset, the paired seed is the causal replication unit for matched C/D contrasts unless a later protocol prospectively establishes a different valid unit.

Windows, detector events, rules, opportunities, bootstrap replicates, and explanation records must not be pooled and treated as independent causal replicates.

Each scenario and dataset must be reported separately before any pooled or cross-environment summary.

Any pooled analysis is secondary and requires a prospectively defensible common estimand. Heterogeneous scenario/dataset results must remain visible rather than being averaged into an apparently universal effect.

Five seeds in one scenario are stochastic replication, not environmental replication.

## Claim-boundary rules

The primary controlled CICIDS2017 pseudo-chronological source-regime result remains what it is. Stage-10 may broaden evidence only prospectively.

Do not claim:

- natural production drift from controlled pseudo-chronology;
- universal trigger superiority;
- universal symbolic benefit;
- architecture invariance without the conditional backbone replication;
- operational/deployment robustness without the conditional operational stress package;
- analyst usefulness without direct evidence;
- broad environmental generalization from CICIDS2017 alone;
- a new confirmatory family selected after outcome access.

Gradual, attack-pattern/real-concept, unseen/evolving-attack, and second-dataset evidence are required before broad environmental claims. If a planned replication is scientifically indefensible, the manuscript claim must narrow rather than manufacture a scenario.

## Inference and preservation

- No Stage-10 result replaces an accepted primary Stage-8/9 result.
- Primary n=5 is not extended after inspection to chase significance.
- Trigger-superiority claims require separately frozen comparator-clock sensitivity.
- New tests chosen after outcome access are not prospectively confirmatory.
- Null, mixed, or unfavorable replication findings remain valid evidence and are preserved.
- Failed or superseded Stage-10 audit/execution attempts are write-once and retained.
- Stage-specific outcomes must remain inaccessible until their protocol freeze is committed.
