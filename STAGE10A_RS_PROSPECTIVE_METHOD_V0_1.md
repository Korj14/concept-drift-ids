## Stop and reporting rules

1. **Prospective §R compliance is not yet complete.** User approval of the R/S structure authorizes development only. Before any R treatment, specify and register the S variant(s), independent confirmation mechanism, lifecycle/horizon/precision analysis, endpoints and inference; obtain scientific review that the joint R/S execution plan fulfills plan §R's 'before the next experiment' language. If S cannot be methodologically defined or scheduled, stop rather than quietly run R alone. Whether S has to execute alongside R or may execute later must be resolved in the signed freeze.
2. S may reuse sealed R Phase-A neural/control evidence, never produce competing neural trajectories to favor a symbolic variant. At most four frozen primary symbolic opportunities in R; S opportunity/event equivalence and censoring must be declared before access. Failed/invalid attempts are write-once retained; unfavorable effects and null findings are valid. No new seeds chosen after results.
3. Explicitly refresh the literature/novelty assessment before frozen S method approval and before major downstream replications. No natural production drift, universal trigger advantage, environmental generalization or architecture-invariant claim from GoldenEye alone.
4. G2 real-source custody, runtime witness, append/fsync escrow, original/child checkpoint protection and read-only Phase B are separate engineering gates. G3 is a separate versioned exact-scope RELEASE. G4 real five-seed raw-trace integrity is checked after authorized capture and before unblinding.

**Next scientific deliverable:** a single prespecified S protocol plus machine-readable variant registry resolving the explicit decisions above and a hostile-review signoff. Until then this is not a locked experimental treatment. No real models, C/D runs or outcomes were accessed in drafting this document.
